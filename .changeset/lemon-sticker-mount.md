Stick a sheet to something and peel it off. `mount` lays a sheet onto an object along geodesics, so it hugs a curved surface. The object can be the built-in lemon (procedural, no asset) or any `.glb` you host. A new `sticker` behavior peels it with a tight bend at the front and a straight flap, adhesive that catches and lets go (`tack`), a clean snap free, and a flight away off the side of the shot. `surface.dieCut` cuts a sheet to its art's own outline with a margin of backing. Every sticker on the object can be clicked and peeled by hand, and clicking the object brings back the ones that flew away. An uploaded `.glb` is read in the pose it is drawn in, and stickers laid on thin or open parts of it overhang cleanly instead of crumpling. The new `peeling-sticker` preset, first in the list, puts it all together under raking light: real brand marks as example stickers (`stickerArt`), pores pressed through the vinyl, and a glossier patch of skin left where the sticker was.
