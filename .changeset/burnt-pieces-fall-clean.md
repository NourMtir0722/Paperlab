A piece that a `<Paper damage>` burn cuts loose from a cloth sheet now falls away cleanly.

It always fell, because every spring across the cut breaks. But on a cut one particle wide, each burnt particle on the edge was averaged between the two sides. As the piece dropped, those particles were dragged down the gap after it, and both burnt edges were drawn as streaks. Now each one rides with the side it borders most. The triangles left across the opening gap are hidden once they stretch, so the piece leaves with its own burnt edge.

A sheet that is still in one piece draws exactly as before.

When a burn is played back from an earlier moment, the damage source hands back paper that had burnt away. The sheet is now laid out afresh instead of hauling fallen paper back up through itself.
